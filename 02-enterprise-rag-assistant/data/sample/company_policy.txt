COMPANY POLICY DOCUMENT

Employee Conduct Policy

1. Purpose
This policy outlines the standards of conduct expected from all employees of our organization. All employees are expected to maintain high standards of integrity and professionalism in their work.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and consultants working for the company, regardless of their position or location.

3. Code of Conduct
3.1 Professionalism
Employees are expected to behave professionally at all times. This includes treating colleagues, clients, and partners with respect and dignity.

3.2 Confidentiality
Employees must maintain the confidentiality of all proprietary information, trade secrets, and sensitive data. Unauthorized disclosure of confidential information is grounds for disciplinary action.

3.3 Conflict of Interest
Employees must avoid situations that create a conflict of interest between their personal interests and the company's interests. Any potential conflicts must be disclosed to management.

4. Workplace Policies
4.1 Attendance
Employees are expected to maintain regular attendance and punctuality. Excessive absenteeism or tardiness may result in disciplinary action.

4.2 Dress Code
Business casual attire is expected unless otherwise specified for specific roles or events. Employees should present themselves in a neat and professional manner.

4.3 Workplace Safety
All employees must follow safety protocols and report any hazards immediately. The company is committed to providing a safe work environment.

5. Technology Use
5.1 Company Equipment
Company equipment, including computers, phones, and other devices, should be used primarily for business purposes. Personal use should be minimal and appropriate.

5.2 Internet Usage
Internet access is provided for business purposes. Employees should not access inappropriate or illegal content. The company monitors internet usage.

5.3 Social Media
Employees should not share confidential company information on social media. Posts should reflect professional standards.

6. Leave and Benefits
6.1 Annual Leave
Full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of annual leave per year. Leave requests must be submitted at least two weeks in advance.

6.2 Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. Medical documentation may be required for absences exceeding three days.

6.3 Other Leave
The company provides maternity leave, paternity leave, and other leave types in accordance with local labor laws.

7. Performance and Development
7.1 Performance Reviews
Performance reviews are conducted annually. Employees receive feedback on their performance and discuss development goals.

7.2 Training
The company provides training opportunities to help employees develop their skills. Employees are encouraged to participate in relevant training programs.

8. Disciplinary Action
Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action, including verbal warnings, written warnings, and termination. The severity of the action depends on the nature of the violation.

9. Policy Updates
This policy may be updated periodically. Employees will be notified of any changes. It is the employee's responsibility to stay informed about current policies.

For questions about this policy, please contact HR at hr@company.com.
